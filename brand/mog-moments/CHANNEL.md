# Your TikTok channel

## Name

**Mog Moments** (my pick) · handle **@mogmoments** (backups: @mog.moments, @mogmomentsclips)

Why: "mog" is the word the whole looksmax community uses, so people searching the niche find
it. It's short, it sounds like a brand, and it doesn't pretend to be clavicular himself.
TikTok bans accounts that look like the creator's own (impersonation), so a fan page needs
its own name.

| Jester Files | @jesterfiles | inside joke from chat, cult-y |

```
Daily clavicular clips 🎥 the moments you missed · fan page

Alternatives:
```

The funniest clavicular moments, daily 📈 not affiliated

Keep "fan page" or "not affiliated" in the bio: it protects the account from impersonation reports.

## Profile

- **Picture:** `mog-moments-logo.png`. TikTok crops it to a circle; `circle-preview.png`
  shows how it will look.
- **Link in bio:** clavicular's Kick channel (kick.com/clavicular). Creators are much more likely
  to tolerate or even repost clip pages that send traffic back to them.
- **Look:** all videos use the same classic captions (white + yellow) and the same yellow/black
  as the logo, so the feed reads as one brand.

## Posting plan for the 10 shorts

- 2-3 a day, 3-4 hours apart. Don't post all 10 at once.
- Best first: 01, 03, 08 have the strongest hooks for new viewers.
- Pin the best performer after 48 hours.
- Clavicular runs an official clipping program (`!clipping` / `!apply` in his stream titles).
  Check it: it can pay for clips and gives you permission to post them.
